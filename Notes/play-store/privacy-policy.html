AccountaBuild Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

AccountaBuild ("we", "us") is a group-accountability fitness app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Questions? Email jake@munitor.ai.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and a display name, used to sign you in and identify you to your group.
  • Profile & goals — optional details you enter, such as height, age, current/goal weight, and your weekly calorie and workout targets.
  • Activity logs — the workouts, calories, weight entries, and progress photos you log, plus messages and reactions you post in your groups.
  • Health & fitness data (optional) — if you enable syncing, we read workouts (exercise sessions), nutrition (calories), and body weight from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) to save you from re-entering them. We only read the data types you grant, and only when sync is enabled. We do not write to Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • Diagnostics — basic app/usage events to keep the app working reliably.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: recording your logs, computing your streaks and seasonal rank (MMR), and showing your group's shared progress and leaderboard.
  • To send the notifications you opt into (e.g., streak reminders, team activity, nudges).
  • To keep the app secure and functioning.

We do not sell your personal or health data, and we do not use health data for advertising.

How health data is handled

Health data read from Health Connect / Apple Health is used solely to populate your activity logs inside AccountaBuild. It is stored with your logs in our database (Google Firebase) and shared with your group members only according to your group's visibility settings (see below). You can disable sync at any time in the app's Health settings, and you can revoke access in Health Connect (Android) or the Health app (iOS). Revoking access stops future reads immediately.

Sharing within your groups

AccountaBuild is a group app. Members of a group you join can see the log status, streaks, rank, and shared entries that your group's visibility settings expose. You control which groups you belong to and can leave a group at any time.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging) to run the app. Firebase processes your data on our behalf under Google's terms. We do not share your data with any other third parties except as required to operate the app or comply with law.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account from the app's settings, or email jake@munitor.ai to request deletion. On deletion we remove your account, profile, logs, and health-derived entries from our systems, subject to any legal retention requirements.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by authentication and server-side access rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

AccountaBuild is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
