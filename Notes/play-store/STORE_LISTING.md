## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Fitness is a team sport.

AccountaBuild turns showing up into a game you play with your crew. Log your workouts, calories, and weight in seconds, and everyone on your team sees who showed up today — so nobody quietly falls off.

CLIMB THE RANKS
Every week your consistency earns MMR and moves you through a seasonal rank ladder, from Iron all the way to Challenger. Hit your goals to get promoted; coast and you'll feel the slide. It's the pull of a competitive ladder, aimed at the one thing that actually matters: doing the work.

YOUR CREW KEEPS YOU HONEST
- See your whole group's status for the day at a glance — who's logged, who's at risk, who's on a streak.
- A shared leaderboard ranks the crew by MMR, with movement, streaks, and an at-risk flag.
- Cheer a teammate's workout, nudge someone who's slipping, and talk it out in group chat.

LOG IN SECONDS
- Calories, workouts, and weight — a couple taps each.
- Connect Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) to auto-import workouts, weight, and nutrition. No more clunky double-entry — synced data is de-duplicated automatically.
- Set weekly goals that power your dashboard and your rank.

BUILT FOR GROUPS
Create a group, share a join code, and bring your friends. Groups with a few active members keep streaks going far longer than going it alone.

WHY IT WORKS
People are far more likely to reach a goal when they've committed to someone else. AccountaBuild is designed entirely around that — shared goals, visible follow-through, and a little friendly competition.

Start your first week in about a minute. Bring your crew. Climb.
```
