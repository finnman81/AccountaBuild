# AccountaBuild — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 August 2026_

AccountaBuild is a fitness accountability app you use with a small group of
people you know. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what we
never do with it.

Contact: **support@munitor.ai**

---

## What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address and display name, so you can sign in
and your group can recognise you.

**Fitness data you log.** Workouts, calorie entries, body weight, and progress
photos — either entered by hand or, with your permission, read from Apple Health
or Health Connect.

**Health app data (optional).** If you enable syncing, we read workouts,
nutrition, and body weight from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android).
You can turn this off at any time in Settings, and revoke it entirely from the
Health app itself.

**Group content.** Messages, photos, and reactions you post to your groups.

**Technical data.** Crash reports and performance timings (via Sentry), plus
basic app-health information such as app version and last-opened time, used to
find and fix bugs.

---

## How we use it

- To show your progress and calculate your Fitness Points and rank
- To show your group the activity you've chosen to share with them
- To send notifications you've enabled (reminders, cheers, milestones)
- To diagnose crashes and performance problems

That is the complete list.

---

## What we never do

- **We never sell your data.** To anyone, ever.
- **We never use health data for advertising.** The app contains no advertising.
- **We never share health data with third parties** beyond the infrastructure
  needed to run the app (see below).
- **We never share your body weight with your group.** Teammates see your
  workouts, streaks, points and rank — never your weight.

---

## Who can see what

**Your group** can see: your display name and photo, your logs (workouts,
calorie-logging activity, weigh-in *frequency*), your streaks, rank, Fitness
Points, badges, and anything you post in group chat or photos.

**Your group cannot see**: your body weight, your email address, your goal
targets, or any data from a group you're not both in.

**We** can see the above plus your email, for support and moderation.

---

## Service providers

- **Google Firebase** — authentication, database, file storage, and server
  functions. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure.
- **Expo** — push notification delivery and app updates.
- **Sentry** — crash and performance reporting.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for
their own purposes.

---

## Your choices and rights

**Delete your account.** Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes
your profile, goals, logs, weigh-ins, points history, badges, and photos.
Messages you posted in group chats remain so conversations still make sense, but
they are detached from your name and identity.

**Turn off health syncing.** Settings → Health, or revoke permission in the
Apple Health / Health Connect app.

**Control notifications.** Settings → Notifications, per category.

**Block and report.** Press and hold any message, log, or photo.

If you'd like a copy of your data or have any other request, email
**support@munitor.ai**.

---

## Data retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account it is
removed immediately, other than anonymised chat messages as described above.
Crash reports are retained for 90 days.

## Children

AccountaBuild is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll tell you in the app before the change
takes effect.
